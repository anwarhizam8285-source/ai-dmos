Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Meta Ads account already?

You need a Meta (Facebook) Business account with ad account access to connect AI-DMOS and launch campaigns. Content generation and the knowledge base work without one.

Will AI-DMOS spend my ad budget without asking?

No. Every campaign AI-DMOS creates on Meta is launched in a PAUSED state. You review it in Meta Ads Manager and activate it yourself when you're ready to spend.

Can I undo an AI-applied optimization?

Yes - any applied recommendation can be undone within 24 hours, restoring the exact previous value (budget, targeting, or bid strategy).

Does AI-DMOS write in Bahasa Malaysia?

Yes. Claude adapts tone and language to your target market, including bilingual Bahasa Malaysia/English copy for Malaysian audiences.

How is billing handled?

Pricing shown on the pricing page reflects list prices per plan. Automated payment processing is not live yet - during this stage, accounts and billing are handled manually. Your dashboard shows your plan and usage, not an automated invoice.

What happens to my knowledge base and campaign data?

Everything is stored per-company in your own isolated data namespace - other accounts never see your knowledge base, campaigns, or performance data.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Since billing is currently handled manually (see above), reach out and we'll take care of it - no lock-in contracts.
